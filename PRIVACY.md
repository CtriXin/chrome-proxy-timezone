# Privacy Policy for Proxy & Timezone

Last updated: April 12, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This extension respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you share with us.

## 2. Information We Collect
We do **not** collect or store any personal data on our servers. The extension processes the following information locally on your device:
- **Proxy configurations**: Saved in Chrome's local storage.
- **Timezone settings**: Used only for browser emulation.

## 3. Network Diagnostics
When you use the diagnostic features (e.g., checking IP risk), the extension sends a request to a Cloudflare Worker node. 
- If you use the default settings, these requests are handled by a private node.
- We do not log or sell any information passing through these diagnostic requests.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We may utilize third-party APIs such as AbuseIPDB for IP risk assessment. Your IP address is shared with these services solely for the purpose of the diagnostic function.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via GitHub.
